Reconstructed EXB01 remote circuit

KEY01-B60 V2 · 2024.09.26 · Unpowered measurements

Layout groups the two button contacts on the left and the two LED cathode contacts on the right. Black/sleeve is not confirmed ground; white/ring 1 remains the ground candidate.

Diode checks: R1 A → LED2 → green/ring 2: 2.57 V.
R3 A → LED1 → white/ring 1: 2.56 V. Each LED lights separately.

Mapped, with evidence limits
  • R1 = 992 Ω · R2 = 99.6 Ω · R3 = 508 Ω · C1 = 98.7 nF, measured in circuit.
  • KEY1 joins its left/right nets when pressed; each side has two duplicate legs.
  • Resistor-to-LED links are supported by resistance matching and individual diode-path illumination; direct opposite-face continuity was impractical.

Green/ring 2 is an external midpoint, so the two LED branches need not carry equal current. No wire is assigned as ground or supply. KVM-side voltages, input numbering, and GPIO compatibility remain unknown. This is a reconstructed circuit, not a manufacturer schematic.
